SYNTHETIC RENTAL AGREEMENT — demonstration only; not a real contract or legal advice.

Section 1: Deposit
The tenant pays a refundable security deposit of INR 80,000. The deposit is returned after handover, subject to documented unpaid rent, utility charges, or damage deductions described in this agreement.

Section 2: Notice period
The tenant may end the tenancy by giving 60 days of written notice to the owner.

Section 3: Monthly payments
Monthly rent is INR 30,000. Maintenance is INR 2,000 per month and is paid separately from rent.
